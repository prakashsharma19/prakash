What is Open Journal Systems (OJS)?

Open Journal Systems (OJS) is an open-source software designed to manage and publish peer-reviewed academic journals. It helps in streamlining the editorial process, article submission, and publication workflow.

Why Choose OJS?

OJS provides numerous benefits, such as:

  • Automated manuscript submission and peer review management
  • Customizable themes and responsive designs
  • Integration with Google Scholar for better visibility
  • Efficient editorial tracking and workflow management
  • Secure access control and role-based permissions
